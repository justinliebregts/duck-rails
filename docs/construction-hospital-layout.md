November 4, 2026. User requested top-down layouts for an under-construction skyscraper and a large hospital. This study assumes both landmarks share one new block; that pairing and all exact footprints remain proposals pending drawing review. No Unity assets changed.

## Three options

- **A — Direct access (recommended):** rectangular construction tower west of the railway and H-shaped hospital east. Entrances face the train; separate rear exits connect to service loops. Construction delivery yard and hospital ambulance court occupy the departure third; site offices, materials and visitor parking occupy the approach third. Best initial option for readable returns and split-team exploration.
- **B — Courtyard loops:** L-shaped construction podium around a work court; U-shaped hospital around a planted entry courtyard. More enclosed exploration, with longer interior routes and rear service exits.
- **C — Staggered campus:** tower slightly toward departure and hospital slightly toward approach, keeping both mainly central. Opposing staging and ambulance courts create a more asymmetric street composition.

## Shared drawing constraints

Use the existing 96 x 160 m study envelope, +Z departure, a straight central railway and a proposed clear perimeter strip. Preserve railway clearance and flat seams. Landmarks mainly occupy the middle third; supporting yards, parking, utility buildings and landscaping extend into both outer thirds. Ground-floor cutaways are schematic, not measured construction plans.

Provide two stair cores/return routes for the tower and a hospital circulation loop with front and service exits. Crane and scaffolding remain clear of the railway. Suggested pedestrian crossings lie beyond either end of the stopped train; moving-train crossing safety requires later review.

Tower height, playable floor count, upper-floor layouts, hospital basement/roof access, exact dimensions and enemy coverage are undecided. The skyscraper should read as tall from the train, but this drawing does not authorize a large stack of playable floors or functional crane/elevator mechanics. Spawn counts and navigation changes require an explicit implementation pass after layout approval.
