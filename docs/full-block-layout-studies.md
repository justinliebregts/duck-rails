# Full-block layout studies

Proposed top-down composition for approval. North is departure, south is arrival. Each row spans the west and east sides of the railway. The drawings are schematic. Fit outer landmarks into roughly Z 8–45 and 115–152 of the 160 m block, tapering to flat seams. Keep the installed track envelope, terrain connections and stop. Asterisks mark multi-level or multi-building precincts condensed for the diagram. Existing footprint and level take precedence over schematic placement.
