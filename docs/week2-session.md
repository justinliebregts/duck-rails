## Playing the encounter

Start from Login with DesktopPlayer. The existing public platform and private code terminal still launch runs. The carriage starts at z=70 with 10 fuel and two each of bats, empty four-slot bags, walkies, flashlight helmets, night-vision goggles, and flashlights. Supplies belong to the crew. Late arrivals share them.

- Desktop: look at a train control and press E; STOP/GO toggles without holding. The existing Tab controls remain available for development. VR: grip the lever with an empty hand near its handle, push forward until it snaps to GO, and keep gripping. Pull back to snap OFF while keeping your grip; push forward again to resume. Release also springs back to OFF and brakes. Only one hand/player can hold it; desktop drive requests are ignored during that hold. Horn uses a physical hold button; bridge retains touch activation.
- The maintenance depot is to the right around z=170–195. Eight ordinary, grabbable coal chunks sit on crates. Coal can use the existing bag inventory. Release a chunk into the open furnace for five fuel; fuel caps at 40 and burns at the existing 0.025 per metre. No idle consumption.
- Two existing zombie minions patrol the authored block. Existing melee knockback/retreat is reused. Their hard boundary stays east of the train corridor, including after knockback. Navigation uses a saved crawlgraph; it is never baked at runtime.
- One hit downs a player. They crawl at 0.65 m/s and cannot grab or operate controls. Hold a hand nearby continuously for three seconds to revive; leaving resets progress. Multiple rescuers do not speed it up. Both involved players hear a rising tone. Protection lasts three seconds afterward.
- The existing flare launches immediately on downing and burns for 30 seconds. Repeat downs can launch another after a 45-second cooldown. If all connected crew remain down for five seconds, the run fails. There is no bleed-out or timed individual recovery.
- At the permanent radio station, keep a VR hand against the TALK button, or aim at it and hold left-click on desktop. Releasing stops station transmission; T/use proximity activation is removed. It uses the same crew voice channel and audio buffers as the handheld walkies. The helmets retain only their existing flashlight functionality.
- Warning signs at z=440 and 500 precede the raised bridge at z=550. Brake manually. The external button is to the right at (11, 1.25, 530). Pressing it starts a shared 60-second lowering timer and two additional enemies. Touching the bridge with the train's front before it is fully down explodes the train and fails everyone.
- When ready, board and drive onto the bridge. The front of the carriage (centre + five metres) crossing bridge entry locks results. Standing or downed connected players whose body/head is in the existing carriage volume escape. At least one escape means SUCCESS. Off-train or disconnected participants get Didn't make it. Late joiners before the ending are eligible under the same rule.
- Left-behind players are brought aboard after the snapshot without being revived or gaining escape credit. The train automatically crosses, travels through the tunnel, and emerges into dawn. The ending has enough fuel for its fixed duration. Falls return players aboard after two seconds without downing them or changing results.
- Both outcomes show the same world-space board for 32 seconds, then use the existing coordinated public/private return. The board shows each participant's status, coal, rescues, downs, honks, and an award. Gameplay stats freeze at finish; ending honks still sound. Private rooms retain their code for replay.

These durations and difficulty settings are implementation defaults chosen under the user's authorization to use sensible defaults. They are tuning values, not a claim of headset playtest acceptance.
