[Current bridge evidence](bridge-summary.md) covers cuts during the fall, reconnects after settling, master departure, all three impact angles, fresh reset, actual success and passenger recovery. The visible section falls finish by approximately 2.96 seconds (`4 * .14 + 2.4`); results appear at 3.2 seconds. Observed ordinary recoveries took roughly four to five seconds. A disconnect initiated after impact therefore cannot be assumed to establish arrival during falling. This narrow timing case is optional additional coverage, not an invented prerequisite for the already-recorded during-disconnect acceptance.
