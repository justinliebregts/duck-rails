# Running expedition arrivals, November 20, 2026

**PASS on three authenticated desktop clients after one gameplay fix.** Tested on one workstation with Unity 6000.3.24f1, three distinct accounts, real Photon US and simulated DuckPlayer rigs. A used the main editor. B and C used the Player2 and Player3 MPPM processes. All clients used normal Login. Real public platform departures and private staging launches each started with two players, leaving the third outside the run.

The first two rooms ran on main `1579de836c1b6ceefcd037fcf5dcbc206ab311a3` with the existing uncommitted gameplay, scene, asset and project changes. These included the two PhotonSession departure-horn hunks and local changes to RailExpedition, RailAdminTools, RailTestTools, RailAudio, PhotonGrabbable, NewPlayer, PauseMenu and ProjectSettings. The two rerun rooms used the same working tree plus the reserved-actor fix, committed separately as `bb87c3ddf3df2e718aab9dbbc6b69b8d1a5b3a6b`. All three post-fix editors reported gameplay assembly `a885c0fa-3ef5-46ff-ac57-8f150db33c3c`.

## Cases

- **PASS — Public and private fresh arrivals.** C entered the five-digit code of an already-moving expedition. The first public request came at 243.94 m and 4 m/s, beyond the first scavenging block. In private, A was down and B wore a loaded bag. C arrived aboard in both room types. C selected actual room coal with the simulated palm, picked it up, released it through the firebox and received a host-confirmed increment from zero to one in each room type.
- **PASS — Four off-train transport reconnects.** The clients established an off-train body position before inducing transport loss, observed the offline state, restored transport and let PhotonSession recover. The public peer and former host were ready aboard after 13.20 s and 12.51 s. The private peer and former host took 13.19 s and 11.96 s. Each time begins at off-train fixture placement. The public former host disconnected at 4 m/s with 10.73 fuel. All clients subsequently agreed the fuel was empty and the train had stopped at 487.59 m. The other three recoveries were on moving trains.
- **FAIL before fix, PASS after fix — Full process restart in a reserved slot.** A cold public or private code entry used an ordinary join. The private baseline failed twice while the same authenticated account still had a reserved actor. The second fresh process recorded Photon error 32749 about 63 s after shutdown. After the reservation expired, the identical code succeeded with a new actor. The fix retries an explicit code join through Photon’s rejoin operation only when Photon identifies the account’s own inactive actor. An active duplicate, closed room and full room continue through the normal failure path.
- **PASS — Public cold rerun.** MPPM shut down B’s off-train client and launched a new process. Its process-lifetime marker was absent. Normal Login reached a compound, then B entered the expedition code. The ordinary join returned 32749 and the rejoin succeeded 0.82 s later. B was world-ready aboard after 75.10 s, at 641.04 m and 4 m/s.
- **PASS — Private cold rerun.** The same sequence restored B aboard in 75.39 s at 413.37 m and 4 m/s. Photon returned 32749, then accepted the reserved-actor rejoin 0.82 s later. Both cold reruns preserved actor 2 and avatar 2001. Each showed one worn basic bag with coal, gold, flashlight and an empty slot, one worn helmet and the two original flashlight grants.
- **PASS — Midpoint shop arrival and interaction.** A and B stood on the shop floor. C joined aboard the stopped train at 430.42 m, reached the shop by fixture placement, and selected, grabbed and released the actual flashlight display. No coins were spent. Natural walking to the shelf and purchase delivery were outside this check.
- **PASS — Moving bridge and defenders.** The stock hand fixture acquired the actual bridge lever, held it forward and published its normal Photon heartbeat. C joined mid-hold, aboard, seeing 10.896 s of retained work and the same two defender views, 128 and 129. All three then saw reinforcement 130. After release at 56.86 s, the train collided with the partially raised bridge. A disconnected participant recovered into the retained Ending with the collapse already visible and no replayed explosion burst. The burst check inspected the effect instance.
- **PASS — Refusal of fresh joins.** During a public all-down Ending and a private Returning phase after the bridge collision, the outside account received Photon 32764 and the game message “This expedition is ending. Join after the crew returns.” Each reached a compound. Neither entered the finished expedition.

## Setup and remaining coverage

The fixtures placed rigs on the real public platform, off the carriage, at coal and beside the shelf. They aligned a simulated palm for the coal grab and supplied release velocity through the firebox mouth. B’s bag and helmet used game delivery/equip paths without a purchase. The editor admin coal button provided fuel for the approach. The bridge shortcut positioned the train for the finale, the stock lever fixture positioned the operator, and a self-revive reset the operator after the down-state observation. The server controlled arrivals, actor reservations, shared state, fuel burn, coal receipts, room closure and rejoin. All clock readings use UTC. The raw export omits the idle interval while Windows process-inventory access was awaiting approval. The cold runs used Unity’s individual virtual-player lifecycle controls.
