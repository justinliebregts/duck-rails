Unity 6000.3.24f1, one workstation, three authenticated accounts on Photon US. A ran the main editor, B and C ran Player2 and Player3. All used normal Login, desktop DuckPlayer rigs and the production session, bridge, enemy and train code. The original run tested main `bb87c3ddf3df2e718aab9dbbc6b69b8d1a5b3a6b` with the existing uncommitted changes. All three reported gameplay assembly `a885c0fa-3ef5-46ff-ac57-8f150db33c3c`.

## Bridge and two natural finishes

- **PASS — accumulated hold, handoffs and late join.** A acquired the physical lever in a public expedition. The master moved from A to B at 14.119 s of work. C joined aboard during lowering and was ready at 21.126 s. A released at 28.907 s. All three stopped at 29.197 s with both original defenders and no wave. After a 21.24 s pause, A resumed as non-master. Each client first saw one reinforcement between 30.205 and 30.428 s and agreed on its view 101. B handed authority to C at 34.024 s during the hold. Two more releases retained 37.221 and 39.567 s. All three saw the bridge reach exactly 60.000 s, stop moving and retain one wave. Their first 60-second samples fell within 0.145 s of one another.
- **PASS — defenders and idle base enemies.** The two original tower defenders (views 99 and 100) and midpoint zombie 101 all reached the cabinet. Across peers their minimum measured distances were below 0.40 m. Base zombies already belonging to the run used positions from the fortress spawn set. While idle they initially had no cabinet goal. During lowering views 83 and 88 approached, and visible player contact took precedence through normal Chase. After A became a non-master, fresh placements during the 29.197 s pause produced ordinary wandering and no cabinet request. On resume enemies again acquired the cabinet goal.
- **PASS — finish an existing path after release.** C was master when A released and withdrew from the cabinet at 39.252 s. View 83 was on a real path from the northwest tower. It continued while the lever was silent, finished all 34 nodes of path revision 28 and came within 0.18 m of the cabinet approach. Still in Wander with no player sighting, it next walked toward a new goal 22.66 m away. Defenders continued to seek the control after release. [Authoritative path samples](bridge-return-path.tsv.gz)
- **PASS — normal victory, crew destination and replay reset.** With all three aboard, A requested normal train movement. The train crossed the lowered bridge, the game recorded `Bridge crossed` and the crew completed its public return to Compound 59075. All three then boarded its public platform together and transferred into expedition 23165. A readiness failure on that departure is recorded below. After A recovered through network reconnect, the route had zero bridge work and zero wave before the new physical hold. The second bridge also completed naturally at 60.000 s.
