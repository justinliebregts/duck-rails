# Subway compound V2

Current scene: `Assets/Scenes/Compound.unity`. Read `docs/subway-compound.md` for confirmed direction, measured layout, checks and limitations.

This revision narrows the occupied floor to 18 x 24 m, adds recessed former train boarding edges behind makeshift panels and narrow viewing slits, and raises the original ceiling to 7 m above low shelters. A transparent wire guard keeps the track strips inaccessible above and between the panels. Six original pillars and selected lit beams/pipes establish the taller terminal structure.

Station info and the walk-through supplies store face the shared centre. Retain the shallow broken fountain, sealed surface stairs, platform departure and private keypad. Existing runtime systems and rig proportions are unchanged.

Edit the seven nested prefabs; installation reuses these saved assets. The `Subway` folder preserves the first blockout. Do not regenerate edited assets. Dimensions remain trial values pending user/headset review. Audio anchors are placeholders; tunnel ambience has not been implemented.
